# Homework 01 — UX/UI Project Discovery

**Course:** UX/UI Design — PTE MIK  
**Project:** AutoPiac — A User-Friendly Car Listing Experience  
**FigJam:** https://www.figma.com/board/8M1vecaiwW7U9MpYBbuok2  
**Status:** Initial framing; user research not yet conducted.

## 1. Problem
When private vehicle owners in Hungary try to post a vehicle advertisement online, lengthy forms, unclear vehicle specifications, or insufficient feedback **may** lead to errors, confusion, or abandoned listings. This is an **unvalidated problem hypothesis**, not a research finding.

## 2. Target Users
- Individual vehicle owners in Hungary who wish to sell their cars.
- First-time sellers with limited experience creating advertisements.
- People using mobile and desktop devices.

## 3. User Goal
Create an accurate car listing quickly, understand required fields, upload appropriate photographs, correct mistakes, and submit confidently.

## 4. Business Goal
Improve listing completion, reduce avoidable errors, and raise the quality of vehicle advertisements.

## 5. Constraints
- Responsive mobile and desktop design.
- Understandable vehicle terminology.
- Clearly marked required fields and photo requirements.
- Accessible form controls and readable feedback.
- Figma clickable prototype rather than a fully deployed service.

## 6. One-Sentence Problem Statement
Private car sellers in Hungary need a straightforward way to create complete and accurate online listings, but complicated form flows and unclear information requirements may prevent confident completion.

## 7. Measurable Success Criterion
**Proposed target:** At least **2 of 3** usability test participants complete the main listing task independently. Track completion time, mistakes, hesitation, and requests for assistance. No results exist yet.

## 8. Assumptions and Evidence Plan
| Assumption | Status | Validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long vehicle-listing forms confuse some private sellers. | Hypothesis | Interview and observe actual sellers |
| Step-by-step guidance improves clarity. | Design hypothesis | Compare usability feedback |
| Clear validation helps users recover from mistakes. | Design proposal | Observe error recovery in prototype |
| Mobile photograph upload needs particular attention. | Product assumption | Interview and task observation |

**Highest-risk assumption:** Car sellers actually experience serious usability friction during listing creation.

## 9. Initial Pitch
AutoPiac is an automotive marketplace concept for the Hungarian market. This UX/UI project explores how a guided, accessible advertisement creation experience could make selling a vehicle easier and less error-prone. The process will include real user research, information architecture, Figma wireframes and prototype, usability testing, and evidence-based iteration.
