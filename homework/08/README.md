## Usability Test Objective
Determine whether private sellers can complete the AutoPiac listing task independently and understand required details, photos, form errors, and publishing confirmation.

## Test Task
"Create a vehicle advertisement for a car you own or know, add the required information and photographs, review everything, correct an error, and submit the listing."

## Participants and Procedure
- Recruit at least **three** relevant adult participants.
- Give each the same task without coaching.
- Record anonymized outcomes: task completion, time, steps causing hesitation, errors, and participant remarks.
- Avoid identifiable personal details, vehicle registration numbers, or unnecessary photographs.

## Success Criteria (Proposed)
- At least **2 of 3** finish without assistance.
- Every observed critical error is documented and assigned a severity.
- Priority fixes are traced to evidence.

## Mini UI Guideline — Initial Proposal
**Typography:** Interface sans-serif, 16 px body baseline and clearly tiered headings.  
**Spacing:** 8 px-based spacing sequence (8/16/24/32 px).  
**Colors:** Neutral surface/background, one high-contrast primary action color, red or equivalent for errors paired with explanatory text, green success paired with text/icon.  
**Fields:** Persistent visible labels, examples when useful, required-state indication beyond color alone.  
**Buttons:** Distinguishable default/hover/focus/disabled states.  
**Contrast:** Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA (normal text 4.5:1; large text 3:1 where applicable).  
**Keyboard:** Visible focus, logical tab sequence, keyboard alternatives for photo reordering.  
**Errors:** A plain-language error summary plus field-linked correction instructions.

Reference: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
