## Prototype Flow

Start Listing → Vehicle Details → Equipment & Condition → Photos → Price & Description → Review → Completion.

The main buttons connect the screens. The Review page also links back to the relevant sections, and separate examples show missing required fields and an insufficient photo count.

## Main Interactions

| Action | Intended behavior | What the Figma prototype shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continue | Move to the next part of the listing | Clickable navigation |
| Back | Return to an earlier step | Clickable navigation |
| Review a section | Open that section to make corrections | Linked section cards |
| Missing information | Explain what needs to be completed | Separate validation example screens |
| Finish | Confirm that the task has ended | Prototype completion screen |

These links are useful for checking the flow. Figma does not save field values, make the checkbox choices functional or publish an advertisement.

## Feedback From a First-Time User

I showed the prototype to my 18-year-old brother, who had never created a vehicle listing. He understood the available navigation and completed the walkthrough without assistance.

His feedback included three points:

1. The overall layout was simple and easy to follow.
2. The car's equipment could not be selected.
3. There was no dedicated damage or replaced-parts section.

The second and third points led to the new Equipment & Condition screen. When he saw the first revision, he liked the change but wanted a longer equipment list. That sample was expanded to 16 features in four groups.
