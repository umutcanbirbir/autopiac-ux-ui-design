# AutoPiac — Follow-Up Feedback on Equipment & Condition

**Notes prepared:** 21 November 2026  
**Participant:** R01, my 18-year-old brother  
**Design reviewed:** The first Equipment & Condition screen with four feature examples

## Feedback

I showed R01 the revised Equipment & Condition screen after adding the features and damage sections he had requested.

He **liked the updated design**, but said the **equipment list could be developed further**. He did not mention any other specific issue during this follow-up. I did not time the session or collect a separate full-task completion measurement.

## What I Changed

I expanded the checklist from four features to 16 examples, organized under four categories:

| Category | Features |
| --- | --- |
| Safety & Driver Assistance | ABS, Adaptive cruise, Lane assist, Blind spot alert |
| Comfort & Interior | Heated seats, Climate control, Power seats, Panoramic roof |
| Infotainment & Connectivity | Navigation, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth |
| Exterior & Parking | LED headlights, Parking sensors, Rear camera, Keyless entry |

I kept the damage-status and repair-notes sections. The [updated Figma screen](https://www.figma.com/design/Po5hpum1mNJ7VnfVc0FTmC?node-id=2007-2) also shows a search-field concept and an option to mention other equipment in the description.

The feature boxes and search field are visual examples, not functional inputs.

## Next Test

I need to find out whether new users can locate equipment in the correct category, understand the damage statuses and still find the page simple to use. The 16-feature version has not yet been retested.

R01 is the same participant as in the original interview, not an additional person.

## Later Prototype Update (22 November 2026)

Following additional feedback that the original checkbox visuals could not be changed, the current design was updated with independent On/Off interactions for all 16 features. The visual search field was removed. This is a newer prototype iteration; it does not change what the participant saw during the original follow-up. The [interactive version](https://www.figma.com/design/Po5hpum1mNJ7VnfVc0FTmC?node-id=2007-2) still needs to be tested in Figma Present mode.
