# R01 — Informal Follow-Up on AutoPiac Equipment & Condition Screen

**Report prepared:** 2026-11-21  
**Participant:** R01, 18 years old, researcher's sibling, first-time prospective seller.  
**Source:** Owner-reported short follow-up reaction, not an independently administered task test.  
**Test duration / exact session timestamp:** Not recorded.  
**Design reviewed:** AutoPiac's first post-interview **Equipment & Condition** static Figma screen with four example equipment options and a panel-status model.

## Reported Reaction

- The participant **liked the revised design**.
- The participant nevertheless felt the **vehicle equipment list could be developed further**.

No other difficulty, measured completion time, specific requested equipment items, independent navigation outcome or verbatim participant quote was reported in this follow-up. **Do not infer them.**

## Interpretation

This feedback supports extending the existing four-option equipment sample with sensible categories and additional features, without abandoning the earlier positive reaction to the simple main journey. It does **not** verify that a redesigned 16-option screen is easier to use.

## Implemented Response

The AutoPiac Figma **Equipment & Condition** screen was updated again to display a four-category, 16-item equipment checklist:

1. **Safety & Driver Assistance:** ABS; Adaptive cruise; Lane assist; Blind spot alert.
2. **Comfort & Interior:** Heated seats; Climate control; Power seats; Panoramic roof.
3. **Infotainment & Connectivity:** Navigation; Apple CarPlay; Android Auto; Bluetooth.
4. **Exterior & Parking:** LED headlights; Parking sensors; Rear camera; Keyless entry.

A search-style placeholder and "Other equipment" prompt were also included. Visual selections are **static Figma representations, not working toggles or search inputs**. The separate Damage & Replacement History block remains.
