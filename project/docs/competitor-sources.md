# AutoPiac — Competitor Research Sources

**Compiled:** 21 November 2026  
**Scope:** Published seller guides for vehicle advertisement creation.

## Használtautó.hu
**Official step-by-step guide:** https://garazs.hasznaltauto.hu/ha-autot-adnal-el/hirdetesfeladas-a-hasznaltauto-hu-n-lepesrol-lepesre/

Documented details:
- Required vehicle fields are marked with an asterisk; some technical fields can auto-populate and remain editable.
- The described process includes vehicle specifications, photographs/documents, photo management, service packages, optional promotional features and submission confirmation.
- Images can be reordered and captioned.
- Package selection is presented during the advertisement creation process.
- The guide describes a post-submission success confirmation.

These points come from Használtautó.hu's published guide. I did not test the current logged-in form.

## mobile.de
**Official selling guidance:** https://www.mobile.de/en/sell/car/

Describes selling a car, preparing photographs, and creating a private-sale listing. I used it as a second comparison for content completeness, not as a direct usability test.

## Hungarian Selling Context
**Hungary's Digital Citizenship official vehicle-sale guidance:** https://dap.gov.hu/eletesemenyek/autot-veszek-vagy-adok-el/autot-adok-el

The guidance recommends an accurate condition description and good photos and notes the role of vehicle history information in seller trust.
